## What was learned (each cost real rows)

1. **Generated skeletons are usually false, and the agent is the best repair tool.** 7/10 wave-1 skeletons had
   level-2/3 decoder holes (payload located through an occurrence that is itself decoded; the outer product of
   the encoding decoded, `v = u`; a decoded product inside a struct-decoded root). The repair recipe (trace the
   failing instance, recover the payload through the provably free occurrence, re-validate, re-emit) is now in
   `WAVE2_PROMPT.md`; it worked 5/5 times when a fable agent applied it.
2. **Random tests do not find these holes; three generators do.** `smallcheck.exhaustive` (all assignments of
   one-generator terms ≤ size 9 and two-generator ≤ 5), `fuzz.closure_fuzz` (pool closed under law-subpattern
   evaluations, products, subterms) and `fuzz.critical_fuzz` (a variable rebuilt as an encoding/product of the
   others, repeated variables). `revalidate.run_tests` runs all of them; the previous 3,000-random-test standard
   passed every false skeleton. Even so, 20,000+ deep tests found holes the validator missed (23354, 28626):
   validate at 20k before an agent spends an hour.
3. **The semantic free model itself is incomplete**: `freemodel.Free` fails 12073 at
   x = ((y◇y)◇y)◇(z◇z) and 6912 at y = (a◇a)◇(a◇(a◇a)) — readings whose nested guard pair is not below the
   msr gate (rail 5f family, in a definition). Lexicographic (enc, dec) size is *not* better (measured on 12
   laws, `gatecmp.py`). The guard-free struct variants (`~` rules) express one level structurally.
4. **Extractor upgrades** (`closedform.py`): mode-aware `unify` (a decoded law-side node inside a struct decode
   is unified as its nested-op value — the 5837/33020/23357 hole), root-side decoded nodes (`rd:` rules — the
   both-compound 24200 hole: the encoding's inner product decoded), guard-free struct variants, pluggable gate.
   24200 and 18137 now validate automatically; 28626 needs all 10 unminimised rules.
5. **Derived identities = quotient laws** (measured, not fixable by modes): 12073 forces
   y◇(y◇(z◇z)) = ((y◇y)◇y)◇(z'◇z') and then K(S) = S for squares — an infinite hierarchy; 27859, 21865, 21866,
   22591 fail on ≤ size-9 one-generator terms the same way. These need a quotient carrier (tags), not the free
   magma.
6. **Bytes**: `squeeze.py` (indent halving, operator spacing, `theorem`→`def`, renames) took 18137 from 23,064 to
   19,705 B (accepted). `macro`/`syntax`/`elab` are banned tokens — 39163's 24.5 KB proof used macros and cannot
   ship; it needs a shorter `CFN` argument.
7. **`dualcert.py` now works both ways** (R-form ↔ L-form) and handles one-line `rhs` proofs; `judge1.py` is
   pid-safe for concurrent agents; `leangen`'s `chk<eq>.py` now tests the dualised law for R-form laws (it
   used to report 3000/3000 spurious fails).
