# Austin Order-5 solving handover

## Status and non-negotiable rails

This is the current continuation plan for the Austin research set.  It is a
research plan, not solver policy: no `sorry`, sampled model, timeout, or
research identifier may enter `solver.py`.  A certificate is promotable only
after the full construction/oracle ladder, a zero-sorry compile under the
deployed limits, and independent official-judge acceptance for every row it
serves.

The image-of-`op` / anchored-carrier restriction is retired.  An op-built
adversary remains in every submagma generated by the inputs, so restriction
cannot repair it.  Before adding a rule or guard, construct a non-vacuous bad
cell and compare its root pair with the offending inner pair: equal pairs are a
functional collision requiring a new state/carrier; different pairs may admit a
general guard, subject to positive-control validation.  See
`automata/gen/NOTES_ANCHORED_CARRIER.md`.

   `GT`/`mxl` for every `msr` max gate.
3. For FDD, derive a **five-way per-rule TR digest** at `(y, J z x)`.  `Pre` and
   `Bdig` are insufficient because one rule has only `tg v = 2`; the digest
   must preserve which rule fired and recover the missing `tg (a1 x)=2` and
   `a1 (a1 x)=y` facts needed for R5.
4. Before Lean work on DFF/DDF, widen the constructed Adig-right census.  Its
   right alternative has not appeared in the existing finite census but cannot
   be assumed impossible.  Either produce a concrete adversary or prove its
   contradiction from the rule-specific digest.

6. For decoded `d`, prove the top dichotomy `J y d = x ∨ a1 d = x`; a decoded

## General model and Lean recipe

For a surviving infinite countermodel: use free terms `M ::= g Nat | J M M`, a
total recursive operation with an explicit size/lexicographic termination
measure, and a finite chain-cell proof partition.  If a machine-checked derived
identity forces quotienting, add an explicit tag/normal-form or quotient carrier
and prove congruence; do not substitute carrier restriction.  The final
counterexample must be a closed generator tuple whose RHS inequation reduces by
constructors/decide.

Every candidate runs: random tests (three seeds), deep 20k (three seeds),
critical/closure, large-junk, level-k descent, per-rule firing census,
constructed adversaries with positive controls, and pair/root functionality.
