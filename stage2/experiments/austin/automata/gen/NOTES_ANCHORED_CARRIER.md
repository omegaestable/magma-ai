## The construction, stated before any measurement

Every model in this family is a term algebra `M ::= g n | J a b | E a b | ...` with `op` a rule system
over it. The law quantifies over **all of `M`**, so the adversary may instantiate `x`,`y`,`z` at terms
that *look* like the model's own outputs but were never produced by `op`. That is the forgery.

**The subalgebra generated by the generators is not all of `M`.** `op` produces
`E u v` only when its free branch fires, `D2 p v` only when the mark fires, and a bare payload
otherwise; so every `E a b` with a rule firing at `(a,b)`, and every `J a b` at all (nothing emits `J`),
is in the term algebra and **not in the image of the evaluation map**.

> **Take the carrier to be that image.** Satisfaction of an equation is inherited by subalgebras
> (downward), so restricting can only remove failures; and every guard is anchored for free, because a
> node of the model's own shape in the image *provably came from the rule that emits it*.

### Lean encoding (decided before measuring, because it constrains the design)

Not a subtype. **A quotient of the free magma**, which is strictly better because it hands the proof a
structural induction on the *derivation*:

```lean
inductive W | gg : Nat -> W | mm : W -> W -> W        -- the free magma on the generators
def ev : W -> M                                       -- ev (gg n) = g n ; ev (mm a b) = op (ev a) (ev b)
def C := Quot (fun a b : W => ev a = ev b)
instance : Magma C := <Quot.lift2 (fun a b => Quot.mk _ (W.mm a b)) ...>
```

* the lift is well defined by `congrArg2 op` — one line, no side conditions;
* `Quot.ind` x3 reduces `EquationLHS C` to
  `forall a b c : W, ev a = op (ev b) (op (op (ev a) (op (ev b) (ev a))) (op (ev c) (ev b)))`,
  i.e. **the law restricted to the image, with a W-term witness for each variable**;
* `Quot.sound` closes it, and `Quot.sound` is an allowed axiom (CLAUDE.md judge statuses);
* `!=` for the eq2 refutation goes through `val := Quot.lift ev (fun _ _ h => h)` and `congrArg val`.

**No identity is needed anywhere**, so the `PLAYBOOK_QUOTIENT.md` objection ("every quotient design starts
from a derived identity, and 11081/12087 force none") does not apply: this quotient is by
*equality of value in M*, which is a congruence by construction, not by a derived law.

### THE IMAGE THEOREM — why this was inevitable, and what it costs

> **Let `M` be a term model with operation `op` and generators `X`. The smallest sub-magma of `M`
> containing `X` is `Im(ev)`, the image of the free magma on `X`. Any carrier that (a) keeps `op`
> unchanged and (b) is a magma containing `X` contains `Im(ev)`. Therefore a failing instance whose
> `x`, `y`, `z` are each `op`-built from generators survives EVERY carrier restriction.**

Proof: a magma is closed under its operation, so a sub-magma containing `X` contains `ev(w)` for every
free-magma term `w`, by induction on `w`. The law is universally quantified, so any triple in the
sub-magma is an instance. QED.

**Consequence, and it is the useful half.** "Restrict the carrier to the terms the model itself builds"
is exactly `Im(ev)` — it is *forced*, there is no smaller choice, and it is **void against any failure
whose witness is `op`-built**. So the escape as stated cannot, on its own, close a cell whose witness the
model constructs itself. It can only delete failures whose witnesses are **forged** — terms of the
model's own shape that `op` never emits.

> **Restatement of the target, which is what the four proofs were actually asking for:
> the image restriction does not remove FAILURES, it removes OBLIGATIONS.**
> A rule that was rejected because it misfires at a forged `(u,v)` is admissible on the image. So the
> anchored carrier is not a repair of a rule set — it is a *licence* to re-admit rules the free carrier
> vetoed. The design loop is therefore: re-rank every rejected rule set on the image and keep the ones
> whose only regressions were at forged pairs.

**And the sharp test for any law is one question**: *is the residual failure's witness `op`-built?*
* op-built  ⇒ the image is void for that law; only a change to `op` (or a quotient, which changes `op`)
  can help.
* forged    ⇒ the image deletes the cell for free.

11081 v20: **op-built.** The image is void for it. Recorded so no one repeats the measurement.

## 0. Summary, stated before the evidence

**The literal question — "do rules rejected on the free carrier become admissible on the image of `op`?"
— has a yes/no answer per rule, and the general answer is NO for the reason that matters:**

> **A carrier restriction can never act as a root-vs-inner position separator.** Not "cannot in a term
> algebra" — *cannot at all*, for a reason that is one line long and needs no measurement.

And the corrected framing, which is the useful half:

> **The premise the five laws all rested on — "the root and the inner position present the same `(u,v)`
> pair, so `op` cannot tell them apart" — is a CHECKABLE CLAIM, and for law 9663 it is FALSE.** The two
> positions present *different* pairs. What was true is only that the *current guard* did not
> distinguish them. Adding one conjunct that mentions the relation between `u` and `v` separates them.
> **Measured below: 9663's dominant residual cell goes to zero.**

So the anchored carrier is not the escape. **The pair diff is.**

## 1. THE SEPARATOR DICHOTOMY  (proof, no measurement needed)

Let `T` be a term algebra with a total `op`, and let `Im ⊆ T` be the submagma generated by the
generators (= the anchored carrier; by the Image Theorem above it is the *smallest* possible carrier
restriction, so this covers every restriction). Let a failing law instance present the argument pair
`p_root = (u1,v1)` at the position that must decode and `p_inner = (u2,v2)` at a position that must
mark, and suppose the model needs the two to behave differently.

**Case 1 — `p_root = p_inner` as pairs of terms.** Then `op(p_root) = op(p_inner)`, in `T` and in every
submagma of `T` that contains them, because **`op` is a function**. No carrier restriction, no
well-formedness invariant, no extra constructor, and no guard changes this. The cell is a **HARD
COLLISION**: the two demands must be made *consistent*, or `op` must be changed upstream so the pairs
stop being equal.

**Case 2 — `p_root != p_inner`.** Then a separating predicate on `(u,v)` **trivially exists** (equality
with `p_root` is one). The only real question is whether a *generalisable* predicate in the model's DSL
does. The cell is a **GUARD GAP**, and it is repaired by a guard, not by a carrier.

**Restriction to `Im` is irrelevant to both branches.** In case 1 it changes nothing; in case 2 nothing
had to change. Its only power is the one the Image Theorem already gave it: **delete failures whose
witnesses are forged, and exactly those.** ∎

> **Corollary, and it retires the target of `DEEP_SESSION_8_AUSTIN_HANDOVER.md` §2e / §3b item 3:**
> "a carrier restricted to the terms the model itself builds, in which a well-formedness invariant acts
> as a root-vs-inner position separator" **does not exist as described**. A well-formedness invariant is
> a unary predicate on carrier elements; the two positions differ in their *pair*, not in the
> well-formedness of their parts, and when they do not differ in their pair nothing separates them.
> The direction that works is the opposite one and it already shipped: **13764 ENRICHED the carrier**
> (a second constructor, more state per node), it did not restrict it.

## 3. THE ROOT FUNCTIONALITY TEST — the only rigorous collision criterion, and it clears 11081

The "GOOD ∩ BAD" overlap I first wrote is **not** a contradiction on its own: a pair occurring in
both a succeeding and a failing chain may be innocent, because the failing chain can fail somewhere
else. Corrected in `gen/sepfind.py`. The rigorous criterion is:

> **Every law instance imposes `op(u_root, v_root) = x` as a HARD requirement.** If two instances
> present the *same* root pair with *different* `x`, the model is refuted **and no change to the root
> rule's guard can repair it** — `op` is a function, so the repair must change the chain-building
> rules upstream so the two pairs separate. That is a proof about the model, and it is the honest
> form of "the root and the inner position are indistinguishable".

### Measured, law 11081 v20, the ported (all-op-built) adversary

```
chains 72,416   failing 984
root pairs seen 71,581      root pairs demanding TWO different values: 0
```

> **11081's root map is a FUNCTION.** There is no root collision at all. A root rule computing the
> correct value **exists as a function**; the only question is whether a DSL predicate expresses its
> domain. **The impossibility theorem in `LEMMA_LIBRARY.md` ("no rule set over a free term algebra")
> is therefore not a statement about the law — it is a statement about the vocabulary that was
> searched.**

### But the localisation is real, and now it has witnesses

Restricting to the position pattern that matters — GOOD = branch fires at the **root** of a
*succeeding* chain, BAD = the same branch fires at an **inner** slot of a *failing* chain:

| branch | GOOD (root, ok) | BAD (inner, fail) | GOOD ∩ BAD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (beta, bare payload) | 70,621 | 312 | **8** |
| 2 (alpha, marked decode) | 0 | 480 | 0 (**vacuous** — branch 2 never fires at a succeeding root) |

The **8 pairs** are the sharp object. They are *literally the same `(u,v)`* at the root of one
instance and at an inner slot of another, and every component is op-built (the adversary is the
ported one). Two consequences, both proofs:

* **No predicate on `(u,v)` separates them.** Not `a2 v`'s shape — *any* predicate. So the
  `LEMMA_LIBRARY.md` sentence "the root and the inner position are indistinguishable in the shape of
  `a2 v`" understates it: they are indistinguishable *tout court*, for these 8 pairs.
* **No carrier restriction removes them**, because a submagma containing the generators contains both
  occurrences (Image Theorem). This is the same conclusion session 8 reached, now with a witness set
  and a reason rather than a sample.

Smallest witness of the 8:

```
u = g0
v = K(F(g2,E(g0,g2)),E(E(g0,g0),g0))
```

**What this does and does not license.** It refutes *"repair branch 1's guard"* for 11081 v20. It does
**not** refute the law: the root map is a function, so the obstruction is that the chain builds a pair
it should not, at the B or C product. That is an upstream change, and it is exactly the kind of change
13764 made. **11081 is not closed by these measurements; it is relocated.**
