### Warning for step 1/2: the naive `encA` is NOT true as stated
`encA : Enc w v → sz w < sz v` fails on the R7 shape. `Enc` shape2 is

arithmetic closes. So the induction needs to case on *that* product too; state the invariant on the
pair (`Enc w v` together with what produced the nested op), not on `Enc` alone.

    chain Q and B  :  9380 pairs,  0 NOT free

    a = (g0*g0), b = ((g0*g0)*(g0*(g0*g0)))  ->  W = op a b = J a b = encB(a2 a, a),  op a W = g0
    (likewise (g0*g1), (g1*g0), (g1*g1))

i.e. `W = J(a, J(a, J(a2 a, a)))` and R1 fires at `(a, W)` returning `a2 a`. So **do NOT try to prove
